# Joiner, mover and leaver procedure

These procedures are for a fictional test organisation. Follow approval and identity controls; do not use them to change real employee accounts without an authorised process.

## Joiner: new employee
1. Receive an approved request from the manager/HR contact.
2. Verify employee name, start date, department, manager and required role.
3. Confirm device and software requirements.
4. Create the service request and link the assigned asset.
5. Create the account using the approved naming convention.
6. Add only role-required security groups.
7. Apply baseline settings and authentication requirements.
8. Prepare the device, updates and required applications.
9. Verify identity at handover; provide access instructions securely.
10. Confirm sign-in, required applications and shared resources.
11. Record asset tag, hostname, assigned user and handover date.
12. Obtain user confirmation and close with validation notes.

## Mover: role or department change
1. Obtain manager/HR approval and effective date.
2. Identify access no longer required.
3. Identify the new role's approved access.
4. Remove obsolete group membership and add only approved new groups.
5. Review application and shared-folder permissions.
6. Confirm access with the user or manager.
7. Update asset ownership/department records if needed.
8. Record changes and approvals in the ticket.

Do not simply add new access and leave old permissions behind.

## Leaver: employee departure
1. Receive an authorised request with exact effective date/time.
2. Confirm approver and process owner.
3. Coordinate timing with manager/HR.
4. Disable the account at the authorised time; revoke sessions/tokens where permitted.
5. Remove group and application access according to policy.
6. Recover laptop, peripherals, access cards and company property.
7. Preserve or transfer business data only under an approved process.
8. Remove/reassign licences according to policy.
9. Update the asset register and record device condition.
10. Validate access removal and document exceptions.
11. Close the ticket when all required tasks are complete.

## Required controls
- Record approval before account or permission changes.
- Prefer role-based groups over ad hoc individual permissions.
- Separate requester and approver where practical.
- Never send passwords in tickets or commit them to Git.
- Record who performed each change and when.
- Escalate urgent or disputed requests rather than guessing.

## Evidence
Capture sanitised request/approval, test-account before/after state, relevant fictional group membership, asset assignment/recovery record and validation result.
